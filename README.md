Seasonal Agriculture Performance Analysis

Project Overview:-

Seasonal Agriculture Performance Analysis is a data analytics project that studies agricultural performance across different seasons, crops, states, and irrigation methods.

The project analyzes agricultural data to identify patterns and relationships related to yield, production, resource usage, environmental conditions, revenue, cost, profit, and disease/pest risk.

Objectives:-

- Clean and prepare the agricultural dataset.
- Analyze agricultural performance across different seasons.
- Compare crop-wise performance and profitability.
- Analyze different irrigation methods.
- Study resource usage and water efficiency.
- Examine environmental factors and their relationship with profit.
- Identify profitable and loss-making farms.
- Generate useful insights for agricultural planning and decision-making.

Key Analysis:-

The project includes:

- Seasonal Profit Analysis

- State-wise Profit Analysis
- Environmental Factors vs Profit
- Profit vs Loss Analysis
- Crop-wise Loss Analysis

Key Findings:-

- Kharif season showed the highest average profitability and yield.
- Zaid season showed comparatively lower profitability.
- Sugarcane showed the highest average profit and yield among the analyzed crops.
- Drip irrigation showed the highest average profit and yield among irrigation methods.
- Rainfed farming showed the highest water efficiency.
- Yield showed a moderate positive relationship with profit.
- A significant proportion of farms were loss-making, highlighting the importance of profitability analysis.
